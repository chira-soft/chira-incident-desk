# Service Offer

## Positioning

CHIRA Incident Desk is a focused technical service for teams that have a reproducible software failure but lack the time or specialist capacity to diagnose and close it safely.

## Ideal customer

- SaaS teams with a blocked release
- Agencies inheriting incomplete code
- Founders with an API, webhook, deployment, or automation failure
- Open-source maintainers funding a clearly scoped issue
- Operations teams facing a repeat incident in n8n, Docker, or a third-party integration

## Engagement boundaries

Accepted work must have:

- A lawful and authorized target
- A reproducible or evidence-backed failure
- Explicit acceptance criteria
- A named technical contact or maintainer
- Agreed payment and payout method
- A review path for the delivered change

CHIRA Incident Desk does not accept credential theft, unauthorized access, malware, evasion, deceptive automation, or work outside the owner's explicit authorization.

## Commercial sequence

1. A short intake is reviewed.
2. A paid diagnosis is proposed when the evidence is sufficient.
3. A repair estimate is issued after reproduction and root-cause analysis.
4. Implementation begins only after written acceptance.
5. Delivery includes evidence and a clear definition of what remains out of scope.
